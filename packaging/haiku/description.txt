Basilisk is a XUL-based web browser built on the Unified XUL Platform (UXP). This is an unofficial community port to Haiku, not supported by the Basilisk developers; please report problems to the Haiku port, not upstream. Optional packages, loaded at run time when installed: mesa (libOSMesa) for WebGL, and ffmpeg6 or ffmpeg8 (libavcodec) for H.264 video and AAC audio; VP8, VP9, AV1 and Opus play without it; myspell (English) or myspell_<language> for spell checking.
